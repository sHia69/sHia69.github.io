About Me

Hello, my name is Hia, pronounced "Haya." I began using Hia while living in Germany because Haya was harder for people to pronounce, but I still prefer my original name. My pronouns are she/her. I am studying Biomedical Engineering Technology (BMET) at St. Clair College, focusing on computer programming and building actual projects. Before that, I studied Computer Programming for one year and achieved Academic Distinction. I am curious about biomedical engineering, software, and health care systems, specifically in how technology can improve patient safety, reliability of the equipment, and the process of working. I enjoy creating coding projects with good functionality and user interface, and I frequently work on Linux. Apart from engineering, I paint and create digital art. It enables me to look at technical tasks differently and emphasizes my conviction that innovation comes where logic and creativity meet. Now I am looking for an opportunity in biomedical engineering, clinical technology, or healthcare support fields where I can use my technical skills combined with my creative side.
